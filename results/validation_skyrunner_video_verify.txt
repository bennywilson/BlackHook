The full-screen field
========================================================================
SceneVelocity carries velocity for dynamic and WPO geometry only. Everything
else - ground, rocks, sky - is black in it, and correctly so: the consuming
shader reconstructs those pixels from the camera matrices. Doing the same thing
to the UNWRITTEN pixels, rather than comparing against the written ones, is what
turns a sparse buffer into a dense field. It is the same calculation as above.
